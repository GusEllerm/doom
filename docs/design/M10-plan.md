0.3. **`S_AdjustSoundParams` (s_sound.c:752-818) — attenuation AND panning, verbatim.** Distance = GG1 approx-euclidean `adx+ady-min(adx,ady)>>1` on **x/y only, no z term** (:767-771; our SfxEvent's z stays informational). ## 3. Deviations, deferred hooks, and open decisions

- **D-10a (M10-05) pitch jitter WITHOUT `M_Random`**: linuxdoom-1.10 burns one menu-stream `M_Random()` per S_StartSound (s_sound.c:330/:340) — adopting it would shift `st_face`/WI stream values and re-bless goldens for a ±1/16 pitch wobble no golden can see. We keep the DISTRIBUTION (saw ±8, other ±16, itemup/tink none — §0.2) from a sound-owned `splitmix(gametic, sfxId, originId)` — deterministic, zero-stream-regression (§0.9 proof obligation), and NOT player-timing-dependent (fixes vanilla's demo-desync class bug for free). Decision of record: DECISIONS.md D-10a.
- **D-10b (M10-05) golden = TS software mix, playback = WebAudio nodes**: OfflineAudioContext runs are NOT stable across browser builds (resampler/panner internals). The goldens certify the PLAN + the exact i_sound.c int law via `renderMix`; the browser path mirrors the same decisions through GainNode chains (linear `v/127` ≡ `vol_lookup` linearity). Deviation surface = WebAudio's own source-node resampler for non-native rates (audible-tolerance item, playtest-checked).
- **D-10c (M10-05) channel count 8** (not 1.10's shipped `snd_channels=3`, m_misc.c:282 — an artifact config; not Chocolate's claim of 8 either (that's the DOS mixer width, i_sound.c:94 = OUR mixer width too): 8 sfx channels with vanilla allocator semantics is the documented middle that keeps firefights layered).
- **D-10d (M10-01) restore `*8`**: 1.10 passes the 0..15 thermo raw (the `/* *8 */` comments, §0.7) — near-silent beyond point-blank; we restore DOS-era `internal = thermo*8`.
- **D-10e (M10-05/06) live per-tic param updates**: the mirror's `I_UpdateSoundParams` is a NO-OP (frozen pan/vol, i_sound.c:675-688); we apply §0.3 per tic (Chocolate behavior, roadmap-exit semantics “attenuation/panning”).
- **D-10f (M10-06) frame-time → tic-time scheduling**: vanilla updates sounds per FRAME (d_main.c:392) on a wall-clock audio device; our sim events are tic-stamped — the driver schedules at the AudioContext time recorded at each tic boundary (ring of 8) with the 100 ms lookahead absorbing rAF jitter.
